# Call Script Cheat Sheet

## The Opening (15 seconds)

> *"Hey [name], it's Sen from MSK Creations. Quick one — how many calls have you missed this week because you're on a job?"*

⬆️ This is the hook. Wait for their answer. Don't rush past it.

## The Pitch (30 seconds)

> *"That's lost work. I've got an AI receptionist that answers your calls 24/7, takes the customer's details, and texts you immediately with their name and number. £97 a month, no contract. I can have it set up for your business today."*

> *"Can I show you? Got 10 minutes?"*

## Objection Handling

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| "Word of mouth is enough" | "People who hear about you still Google you. No answer on that first call means they call your competitor." |
| "Too expensive" | "£97/mo is £3.20 a day. A minimum wage employee costs you £80 a day. Which costs more?" |
| "I'll think about it" | "What specifically do you need to think about? Is it the cost, or whether it actually works?" |
| "My wife handles calls" | "So when she's at the school run or asleep, who's answering?" |
| "Not interested" | "No worries. If you change your mind, I'm Sen with MSK Creations. What's the best number to reach you?" |
| "Send me some info" | "I can do that. But honestly, it's easier to show you in 2 minutes than describe it. Got 10 minutes tomorrow?" |

## The Close

> *"Alright — let's get you set up. I just need your business name and what hours you want the AI to cover. I'll have it live today."*

## What NOT to Do

- ❌ Don't read from a script robotically — use bullet points, talk naturally
- ❌ Don't apologise for calling — you're offering something valuable
- ❌ Don't over-explain the tech — they don't care about "LLM" or "Twilio"
- ❌ Don't take "not interested" personally — they don't know you yet
- ✅ **Do** call them by name
- ✅ **Do** pause after your opening — let them respond
- ✅ **Do** log every call outcome

## Logging

After each call, note:
1. **Name + Business**
2. **Outcome** (not interested / interested / callback / sold)
3. **Objection they raised** (so you can improve)
4. **Follow-up needed?** (yes/no — if yes, when)
