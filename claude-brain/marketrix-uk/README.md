# Marketrix UK — Prospect Tracker

Ready-to-work list of UK companies that could buy Marketrix's research (real research + simulation, ~£9k+/study). Built 2026-08-11 for the Marketrix UK expansion partnership.

## How to use

1. Open `prospects.csv` in Google Sheets / Excel / your MSK-style dashboard.
2. Filter by `Wedge Priority` column: **A-grade first** = sells to hard-to-reach users + has funding.
3. For each A-grade row: open the `Decision to pitch` and `Opening line` columns, find the contact role on LinkedIn (LinkedIn company = `LinkedIn` column; search the company page for the role, e.g. "Head of Product"), and send the opening line.
4. Log the status: `queued → messaged → replied → booked → closed` in the `Status` column.

## The wedge (why this list works)

Marketrix's unfair advantage is recruiting **hard-to-reach audiences** — tradespeople, clinicians, letting agents, surveyors, professionals — the people standard survey panels fail on. Every company on this list *sells to* one of those audiences, so every one of them has a live research question about users they can't easily reach. Each row's `Wedge` names the specific live decision that maps to a study.

## Cadence

- 10–15 new names added/day, 30 outreach messages/day
- Sales cycle for ~£9k research deals: 2–6 weeks. Judge on month 1, not week 1.
- Always agree rev-share + territory terms with the Marketrix founders **before** closing a first deal.

## Data notes

- Companies are real and verified via web search (2026). Contact emails are only listed where actually published on the company site — never invent them.
- `Opening line` is draft copy — personalise before sending.
- Some companies appear in two sectors (e.g. Plentific) — they're one prospect, don't double-message.
