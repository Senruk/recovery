**Real research, run for you. Simulated, so it scales.**

## The Problem
Most product teams get far less primary evidence than they need. Three reasons:
1. **Recruiting is the bottleneck** — Narrow segments, lapsed users, and trade audiences are hardest to reach and matter most
2. **The calendar doesn't fit the decision** — Proper studies take 4–8 weeks; decisions rarely wait
3. **Cost forces rationing** — At $15k–30k/study, teams only ask the biggest questions

---

## What Marketrix Changes
**Properly recruited, properly fielded research on a monthly cadence**, and a **grounded simulation population** to interrogate in between.

### Layer 1: Real Study (The Anchor)
- 20 real in-depth interviews per study (12 AI-moderated + 8 human-moderated)
- Up to 300 screened survey completes from the same audience
- Recruitment fees, participant incentives, transcription, coding, analysis — **all included**
- Readout written for the decision, not the method
- **6 weeks from kickoff to readout**

### Layer 2: Simulation Population (The Multiplier)
- Personas built from your real study evidence (transcripts, verbatims, survey responses)
- Grounded in 3M+ base library across 120k job titles
- Re-grounded after every real study
- **20 follow-up questions answered in days, not another recruit**
- Blind fidelity back-test on every study

## What Simulation Does (And Doesn't) Replace

| Strong At | Not a Substitute For |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Triage — narrowing 20 questions to the 3 worth a real recruit | Decisions that commit money (capacity, print run) |
| Iteration — running the same question 10 ways in an afternoon | Regulatory/technical fact (safety, labelling, claims) |
| Instrument testing — broken screeners before fielding | Observed behaviour in live deployment (velocity, conversion, churn) |
| Extending reach — segments too small/expensive to recruit | |
| Simulated UX research & A/B testing — flows, screens, variants | |

**Our posture:** Real research decides. Simulation decides what to research, and answers everything in between.
