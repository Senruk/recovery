# UK Driving Theory Exam — Study Plan

**Started:** 2026-09-01
**Location:** UK (Wales / South Wales)
**Goal:** Pass the theory test first go. The theory pass is the gate to booking your practical.

---

## What the Test Is

**Two parts, both on the same day, both on a computer:**

| Part | What it is | Duration | Pass mark |
|------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| Multiple choice | 50 questions across 14 topics | 57 minutes | 43/50 (86%) |
| Hazard perception | 14 video clips (19 in Northern Ireland, but Wales = 14) — click when you see a developing hazard | ~20 mins | 44/75 (58%) |

**Total cost (2026):** £23 per test (book at gov.uk/driving-theory-tests)

**Result:** You must pass BOTH parts on the same day. If you fail one, you retake both.

**Validity:** The theory pass certificate lasts **2 years** — you must pass your practical within that window or the theory expires.

---

## The 14 Multiple-Choice Topics

1. **Traffic signs** — regulatory, warning, information
2. **Signals** — traffic lights, amber, red, pedestrian crossings
3. **Lines** — white lines, double, hazard, zig-zag
4. **Road markings** — give way, stop, double white, hatch markings
5. **Hazard awareness** — spotting developing hazards (overlaps with part 2)
6. **Driving essentials** — mirrors, steering, brakes, clutch, gears
7. **Motorway driving** — lanes, hard shoulder, breaks down
8. **Rules of the road** — overtaking, stopping, parking, waiting
9. **Pedestrians** — crossings (pelican, puffin, toucan, zebra), schools, elderly
10. **Cyclists & horse riders** — passing distance, behaviour
11. **Animals** — horses, livestock
12. **Bad weather & driving conditions** — fog, rain, ice, snow, sun glare
13. **Licence & vehicle** — what you can drive, tax, MOT, insurance, seatbelts
14. **Breakdowns & incidents** — what to do, warning triangles, first aid kit

---

## Hazard Perception — How It Works

- 14 video clips, each 10–30 seconds
- A hazard "develops" — a car braking, a pedestrian stepping out, a cyclist swerving
- You click the mouse **as soon as you see the hazard starting to develop**
- **Scoring:** 5 points available per clip. You get points for each click in the "window" before the hazard happens. Early clicks score more; clicks after the hazard are too late (0 points).
- **Max 75 points. Pass 44.**

**Key:** Don't click randomly — wait until you actually see something developing. But don't hesitate either. Practise the timing.

## Study Resources

### Free / low cost

1. **DVSA Official Practice Tests** — the actual questions and hazard clips used in the real test
   - https://www.gov.uk/guidance/practice-theory-tests
   - Free on the DVSA website. **Use this first and most.** It's the only source that matches the real test exactly.

2. **DVSA Official Hazard Perception** — free clips on the DVSA site
   - Practise the timing, not just the watching.

3. **Theory Test Kit app** (iOS/Android) — free version has a limited set; paid unlocks all. ~£4.

4. **YouTube** — "hazard perception clips" walkthroughs. People film their screen and explain what to look for. Good for learning the patterns.

### Paid (worth it if stuck)

- **Theory Test Kit** full version
- **4Tests / PassMeOnline** — big question banks
- **Hazard perception training DVDs** — slow-motion replays of clips with commentary

## 6-Week Study Plan

| Week | Focus | Daily |
|------|-------|-------|
| 1 | Topics 1–4 (signs, signals, lines, markings) + first hazard clips | 30 min theory + 2 clips |
| 2 | Topics 5–8 (hazard awareness, driving essentials, motorway, rules of road) | 30 min theory + 2 clips |
| 3 | Topics 9–12 (pedestrians, cyclists, animals, weather) | 30 min theory + 2 clips |
| 4 | Topics 13–14 (licence/vehicle, breakdowns) + full mock tests | 45 min: 1 full mock |
| 5 | Full mocks, weak-topic review, hazard perception timing | 1 full mock per day |
| 6 | Final mocks, book the test, rest the day before | 1 mock, then stop |

**Daily routine (20–30 mins):**
- 10 questions from a topic you haven't covered
- 2 hazard perception clips (focus on timing)
- Review wrong answers — read the explanation, don't just move on

**Weekend:** One full-length mock (both parts, back to back, no breaks). This is the real test simulation.

---

## Common Pitfalls

- **"I know the rules"** — the test asks for the *most* correct / *best* answer, not just a correct one. Read every option.
- **Clicking too early on hazard perception** — you score more for clicks in the window *before* the hazard, not after.
- **Clicking too late** — once the hazard has happened, it's 0.
- **Stopping on the mock** — the real test is 57 minutes for 50 questions. Practise at speed.
- **Booking too early** — if you fail, you lose 24 hours + the fee. Only book when you're consistently passing mocks.

## Booking the Test

1. Go to https://www.gov.uk/driving-theory-tests
2. Enter your details (need your provisional licence number)
3. Pick a test centre — South Wales options include Cardiff, Caerphilly, Newport, Swansea, Bridgend
4. Book a morning slot if possible — less traffic, more alert
5. **Bring:** provisional licence (paper or card) + proof of address if the name differs

---

## After the Theory Pass

- You get a certificate valid **2 years**
- Book your practical test immediately — slots fill fast, and you want the earliest date you're ready for
- Practise driving between theory and practical — don't just wait
